Who We Are

Christian-founded.
Built for this generation.

UGI was built on a biblical conviction. Every person is made for community, for purpose, and for something greater than themselves. That is the foundation everything here is built on. God said it first in Jeremiah 29:11. We just believed it.

We are a Christian organization, fully and without apology. Scripture and personal testimony are at the center of every single gathering. We also believe that anyone willing to walk through the door deserves a real welcome. Curious, skeptical, or fully committed. Come and see for yourself.

FAQ

Questions we get every time.

Answered honestly, upfront.

Do I have to be Christian to come?

No, but we want you to know what you're walking into. UGI is a Christian organization. Every gathering includes biblical teaching and real personal testimonies. We are not hiding that and we are not apologizing for it. What we are also not doing is pressuring anyone. You are welcome exactly as you are.

Is there a cost?

Uncommon Thursdays is free to attend. If you want to paint during the gathering, bring $20–$30. That's it. Nobody's turned away for not having money.

What actually happens at a Thursday night?

You hang, do something creative together (usually painting), hear a short biblical teaching rooted in Scripture, listen to a real personal testimony, pray together, and leave having actually connected with people. It's real. It's faith-centered. It moves fast.

Can my teen come alone?

Yes. We have trained adult leaders present every single night. Strict two-adult policies are in place and there is no private isolation ever. Your teen is safe.

What's the difference between Uncommon Thursdays and the full UGI launch?

Uncommon Thursdays is our current weekly gathering at Stuart Ceramics. It is a preview. The full UGI launch in August 2026 will be a dedicated 501(c)(3) program in Port St. Lucie built specifically for teens 13 to 18.

How do I get involved or support UGI?

Show up. Bring someone. Volunteer if you're an adult who leads well. Donate if you can. Partner with us if you run a business or organization. We need all of it.

For Parents & Families

We take your trust seriously.

Letting your teen walk into any space takes trust. Here is what we are committed to.

✝️ Christian-Founded, Pressure-Free

Biblical teaching and personal testimonies are always part of our gatherings. That is the point of what we do. Your teen will never be pressured, manipulated, or made to feel unwelcome regardless of where they stand in faith.

🛡️ Trained & Vetted Leadership

Every adult leader is trained in safeguarding, mandatory reporting, and appropriate boundaries. Strict two-adult rules apply. There is no private isolation, ever.

📋 Accountable Operations

UGI is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit with board oversight and documented safety protocols. We are built to be accountable and not just well-intentioned.

📞 We're Reachable

Questions? Want to talk before your teen shows up? Text or call us at (772) 877-5787. You should know exactly who's in the room with your kid.
