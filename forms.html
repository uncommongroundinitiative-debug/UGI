Required Forms

Download, print, complete, and bring both forms to your teen's first event. You can also email them back to us at hello@uncommongroundinitiative.org.

Both forms are required before your teen attends their first UGI event. Fill them out online below — no printing needed. A digital signature is accepted. If you prefer, PDF versions are also available to download and bring with you.

What to do with your forms

1

Download both PDFs

Click the Download buttons above. The forms will save to your phone or computer.

2

Print and fill out by hand

Print both forms and complete them in ink. If you do not have a printer, we can print a copy for you at the event — just let us know ahead of time by texting (772) 877-5787.

3

Bring them to the first event — or email them ahead

Hand the completed forms to a UGI staff member when you arrive, or email them to hello@uncommongroundinitiative.org before your teen's first event.

4

One time only

Forms are on file after the first event. You will not need to re-submit unless your information changes.

Questions about the forms?

We are happy to walk through anything before your teen's first event. Text or email us anytime.
